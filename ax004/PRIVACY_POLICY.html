This Privacy Policy describes how SupaCalendar ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application. By installing or using SupaCalendar, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

SupaCalendar lets you create and manage calendar events locally on your device. The following data stays on your device and is not transmitted to our servers:

  • Event titles, descriptions, dates, times, and reminders
  • Event categories, colors, and notes
  • User preferences (language, theme, default view, notification settings)

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you use the App, we (or our third-party SDKs) may automatically collect:

  • Device information: device model, OS version, screen size, language, country/region, time zone
  • App usage data: features used, screens viewed, session duration, crashes and errors
  • Identifiers: Android Advertising ID (AAID), Firebase Installation ID, app instance ID
  • Performance data: ANR reports, crash logs, network performance metrics
  • Approximate location: derived from IP address (not GPS) to localize holidays and ads

1.3 Permissions Requested

The App requests the following Android permissions:

PermissionPurpose
POST_NOTIFICATIONSShow event reminders
USE_EXACT_ALARM / SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARMTrigger reminders at the exact scheduled time
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETEDRe-register scheduled alarms after device reboot
INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATEFetch holiday data, deliver ads, sync configuration

We do not access your contacts, camera, microphone, precise location, SMS, or call logs.

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain calendar, lunar conversion, and reminder features
  • Localize holidays based on your country/region
  • Schedule and deliver event notifications
  • Display ads (including personalized ads where permitted)
  • Diagnose crashes, monitor performance, and improve stability
  • Analyze feature usage to improve the App
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Advertising

The App is supported by ads. We may show banner, interstitial, native, rewarded, and app-open ads.

  • Personalized ads: when permitted by your consent / region, our ad partners use the AAID and other identifiers to show ads relevant to you.
  • Non-personalized ads: in the EEA, UK, Switzerland, and other regulated regions, we use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain consent or show non-personalized ads where required by law.

You can reset or limit the use of your Android Advertising ID at any time in: Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Google → Ads).

5. Children's Privacy

SupaCalendar is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Data Retention

  • On-device data (events, settings) remains until you delete the data, uninstall the App, or clear app storage.
  • Analytics and crash data are retained by Firebase according to their default retention windows (typically up to 14 months for Analytics, 90 days for Crashlytics breadcrumbs).
  • Ad identifiers are managed by the operating system and ad networks.
